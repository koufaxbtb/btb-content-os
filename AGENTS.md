# Bend The Block Content OS --- Agent Instructions

## Mission

Build a content intelligence system that gets smarter every time Bend
The Block publishes.

Primary objective: help Bend The Block grow toward 100K followers by
improving attention, retention, sharing, follower conversion, and
original-audio reuse through measured experiments.

## Non-negotiables

1.  Never fabricate analytics, benchmarks, trends, or source data.
2.  Clearly separate measured facts from hypotheses and recommendations.
3.  Do not assign arbitrary AI scores to hooks or content.
4.  Prefer simple, inspectable systems before complex infrastructure.
5.  Preserve raw source data. Derived metrics must be reproducible.
6.  Optimize for learning and repeatability, not vanity metrics.
7.  Do not auto-publish content. Human approval remains required.
8.  Treat financial education carefully. Do not frame debt, credit, or
    leverage as risk-free.
9.  Missing data is unknown/null, never zero unless zero was measured.
10. Keep platform-specific performance separate when the same creative
    is cross-posted.

## Product principles

-   Mobile-friendly first.
-   Fast enough to use every day.
-   Every post becomes a data point.
-   Every experiment has a hypothesis, variable, benchmark, result, and
    lesson.
-   Recommendations must show the evidence or state that they are
    hypotheses.
-   Human judgment controls publishing, brand voice, and sensitive
    claims.

## Initial systems

1.  Content Library
2.  Analytics Engine
3.  Experiment Log
4.  Hook Lab
5.  Trend Radar
6.  Culture & Audio Engine

## Build order

Phase 1: data model, seed data, validation. Phase 2: analytics queries
and reports. Phase 3: lightweight dashboard. Phase 4: Hook Lab using
observed patterns. Phase 5: Trend Radar with source provenance. Phase 6:
Culture & Audio tracking.

Do not skip directly to a complex dashboard or autonomous agent
architecture.
