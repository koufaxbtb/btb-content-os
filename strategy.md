# Bend The Block Content Strategy

## North Star

Build a culture, not only an audience. The long-term signal is people
repeating Bend The Block language, sharing content, recreating formats,
using original audio, and telling their own stories.

## Core lanes

1.  Bend The Block Stories --- specific lived stories; story first,
    lesson second.
2.  Old Koufax vs New Koufax --- comedy around old thinking versus new
    thinking.
3.  Road to the Maybach --- measurable credit, income, business, and
    wealth-building journey. The Maybach is the trophy, not the
    foundation.
4.  Credit / Money / Leverage --- document learning without presenting
    debt as risk-free.
5.  Reactive / Trend Content --- timely credit, AI, money, business,
    entrepreneurship, and creator stories, verified before publishing.
6.  Culture / Reusable Audio --- short lines other people can apply to
    their own lives.

## Creative test

Before filming ask: 1. What is the tension? 2. What remains unanswered
after the first sentence? 3. Why should a stranger care? 4. What is the
payoff? 5. Why might somebody share or reuse it?

## Current observed benchmarks

These are observations, not universal targets: - Fatherhood story: 41.8%
skip rate. - Available-credit comedy skit: 47.8% skip rate; 9.16s
average watch time. - Loyalty story: 63.7% skip rate. - Road to the
Maybach Ep. 1 snapshot: 58.2% skip; 6.87s average watch. - Credit-score
skit snapshot: 54.9% skip; 5.50s average watch.

## Current hypotheses

-   Specific personal tension may outperform generic lessons.
-   Comedy may work better when Old Koufax is confidently absurd.
-   Shorter videos may improve retention when no necessary story detail
    is lost.
-   Reusable language may increase sharing and original-audio adoption.
-   Participation prompts may help improve interaction. These are
    hypotheses and must be tested.
