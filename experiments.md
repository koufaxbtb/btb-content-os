# Experiment Framework

Every post should teach us something.

## Required fields

experiment_id, content_id, date_started, hypothesis, primary_variable,
control_or_benchmark, expected_direction, primary_metric,
secondary_metrics, result, lesson, confidence, next_test.

## Rules

1.  Prefer one primary variable per experiment.
2.  Record the benchmark before seeing the result.
3.  Never rewrite the hypothesis after performance is known.
4.  Separate measured result from interpretation.
5.  Small samples support weak conclusions.
6.  One winner does not establish a repeatable format.
7.  One loser does not kill a strong strategic idea.
8.  Retention without sharing or following may still be weak growth.
9.  Re-test promising patterns.

## Backlog

### EXP-001 --- Specific personal tension

Hypothesis: a specific personal tension hook retains better than a
generic lesson. Benchmark: Fatherhood 41.8% skip vs Loyalty 63.7%.

### EXP-002 --- Confidently ridiculous Old Koufax

Hypothesis: Old Koufax performs better when his belief is self-serving
or absurd rather than merely a common misconception. Benchmark:
Available-credit skit at 47.8% skip.

### EXP-003 --- Reusable audio line

Hypothesis: a short universal line inside a personal story increases
shares and eventually audio reuse. Primary metric: original-audio uses.

### EXP-004 --- Participation prompt

Hypothesis: a question viewers can answer about themselves generates
more comments than a generic CTA. Candidate: "If you had access to
\$100K in credit, what would you do with it?"
